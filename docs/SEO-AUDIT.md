## Visual check

Every route at 1440 and 390 px, full page, before and after, pixel-diffed (clock and marquee masked,
reveal animations settled). Share of pixels differing by more than 24/255 in any channel:

- all pages at or below 0.11%, except /portfolio/private-mandates at 390 px (0.44%, text
  anti-aliasing on the body copy, no visible change);
- mean absolute difference at or below 0.59/255 on every capture;
- no change in page height on any capture.

The differences are image recompression in the photographs and sub-pixel text rendering. Side by
side, the captures are indistinguishable.

## What changed

Performance
- Images are pre-generated by `scripts/images.mjs` (sharp): AVIF, WebP and
  a JPEG fallback at a ladder of widths, with content-hashed names under `/img/`. Pages render them
  through `<picture>` with `srcset`/`sizes`, natural `width`/`height`, `loading="lazy"` below the
  fold and `fetchpriority="high"` on the LCP image (hero, detail heroes, the /contact logo). The
  picture wrapper is `display: contents` and the stylesheet's `>img` selectors now match through
  it, so layout is unchanged.
- The header button's logo background (the 1.2 MB `logo.png`, loaded on every desktop page) now
  comes from the same generated set via `image-set()`.
- Both web fonts are preloaded. `font-display: swap` was already set.
- Render headers: `Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable` on `/_next/static/*`,
  `/fonts/*` and `/img/*`; one day on `/og/*` and `/icons/*`; HTML keeps Render's
  `max-age=0, s-maxage=300`. Added `Strict-Transport-Security: max-age=31536000` and
  `Content-Type: application/manifest+json` for the manifest.
- JS target set to the same browser baseline the stylesheet already requires (Chrome/Edge/Firefox
  111, Safari 16.4). No third-party requests exist on any page.
- Inlining the stylesheet (`experimental.inlineCss`) was deployed and measured: home mobile rose to
  98 to 100, every other page fell to 98, and each page carried the CSS twice (in the style tag and
  in the React payload). Reverted in favour of the cached external file.

Crawlability
- `robots.txt` (allow all, sitemap link) and `sitemap.xml` (14 URLs with `lastmod`) generated
  from `src/app/robots.ts` and `src/app/sitemap.ts`.
- Canonicals verified on every page. `/about.html`, `/about/` and `/index.html` still return 200
  because Render serves an existing file before it applies redirect rules (tested: a redirect rule
  for `/about.html` is ignored). Each of those responses carries the canonical to the clean URL,
  which is what search engines use.
- www and http both 301 to `https://bursar.world/`. Unknown paths return a real 404 with the
  site's not-found page.

Metadata
- Unique title and description on every page; titles 19 to 44 characters, descriptions 94 to 156.
  Posts and capability pages got written descriptions (`description` in `_content.ts`, not shown on
  the page) in place of the one-line intros.
- Per-page 1200x630 share cards in `/og/`, rendered by `scripts/og.mjs` from the site's fonts, the
  glass photograph and the page's own heading. `og:type` is `article` on posts with
  `article:published_time`, `modified_time`, `section` and `author`; capability pages are now
  `website`. `og:site_name`, `og:locale`, `twitter:site` (@UseBursar) and image dimensions on all.
- `theme-color`, web app manifest, favicon set (ico, 16/32/48 PNG), 180 px apple-touch-icon and
  192/512 manifest icons.

Structured data (JSON-LD, parsed on every live page)
- Organization (logo, email, `sameAs` X and GitHub) and WebSite on every page.
- FAQPage on the home page, questions and answers taken verbatim from `faq` in `_content.ts`.
- BlogPosting on each post (headline, description, section, datePublished 2026-09-27, author and
  publisher Organization, share image).
- BreadcrumbList on /about, each post and each capability page.

Accessibility and semantics
- /blog and /portfolio skipped from h1 to h3 in the card grid; the card headings are now h2 with
  identical styling.

Content and linking
- Every route is reachable from the header menu or the footer; posts and capabilities are linked
  from the home page and their index pages; /privacy and /terms from the footer. No orphans.
  Card links carry the full card text (heading and summary) as their accessible name.

## What cannot reach 100 without changing copy or design

- Accessibility 90 to 92, and 95 on /privacy and /terms desktop: colour contrast. The muted text
  colour `#786b7d` (and the lighter variants `#807488`, `#837989`, `#85798d`, `#887c8f`,
  `#8d8392`) sits between 3.2:1 and 4.3:1 on the lilac and peach section backgrounds; WCAG AA needs
  4.5:1. Darkening `#786b7d` to about `#685d6c` passes on every background used. The scroll-reveal
  words on the home page are drawn at 20% opacity until they scroll in, which fails by design.
  The footer's Terms and Privacy links are 11 to 13 px tall and stacked 13 px apart, below the
  24 px target size; a fix needs more spacing between them. All three are visible changes, so they
  are left for a design decision.
- Best Practices 96 on /portfolio: the Private mandates card image is stretched to the card
  (`object-fit: fill`, 1.49 source shown at about 1.27). `object-fit: cover` fixes it and also removes
  the distortion, but crops the photo instead of squeezing it.
- SEO 63 on the not-found page: Next marks it `noindex`, which is correct for a 404, and Lighthouse
  scores that as not crawlable.
- Mobile Performance 96 to 99: simulated mobile LCP includes the roughly 160 KB of JavaScript
  (React, Next and the motion layer), because on a real connection it finishes downloading before
  first paint. Every other metric scores 100. Reaching 100 would mean shipping less script, which
  means rebuilding the header menu, accordions and motion without React.
